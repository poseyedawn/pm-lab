# Slice 5 Humanizer copy audit

## Input patterns

The app copy had four recurring problems:

- em dashes appeared in result copy, campaign status, daily copy, About copy, and scenario explanations;
- "Signal decoded" sounded positive even after a missed call;
- several explanations packed too many clauses into one sentence; and
- some lines leaned on slogans such as "red flag, not a jackpot" instead of naming the evidence plainly.

## Draft rewrite

Incorrect result draft:

> You missed the signal. You picked Kill. The correct answer was Ship. The confidence interval stayed above zero. Try again.

Scenario draft:

> A large lift on a tiny sample is unreliable. Only large swings become significant. This can exaggerate the result.

## What still sounded generated

- The repeated "You" sentences felt mechanical.
- "Correct answer" made the experience sound like a school quiz instead of a product judgment game.
- "Try again" did not tell the player what happens next.
- The scenario rewrite removed the actual sample and effect details that make the game credible.

## Final rewrite

Incorrect result:

> That call missed the signal.
>
> Your call: Kill
>
> Better call: Ship
>
> What to notice: The whole confidence interval stays above zero. That makes Ship the stronger call.

Scenario explanation:

> A 15.4% lift on 120 users is a red flag. With a tiny sample, only extreme swings reach significance. That makes the apparent effect look larger than it is. True lift: 0%.

The same plain-language treatment now applies to all ten scenario explanations, campaign status, daily feedback, share text, comments, and About copy. A source scan and a rendered browser scan both found zero em dash and en dash characters.
