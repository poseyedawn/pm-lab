## Slice objective

Make the hub explain the three games before a visitor opens one, remove the remaining shared accessibility failures, and give direct game links the metadata, loading content, discovery files, and browser policies expected from a public product.

## Outcome

The hub now names each game's judgment task, expected duration, and mode. The approved title lockup, full card silhouettes, play controls, dimensional artwork, and full-bleed mobile world remain intact.

The draggable card surface is no longer a keyboard stop and now preserves vertical browsing. Shared Settings closes through its trigger, Escape, or an outside click. Escape and outside click both restore focus to the trigger. Significant and Ship It entry text now passes the serious and critical Axe contrast gate.

Direct game routes now expose specific titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph fields, Twitter fields, and share images. Server responses contain a branded heading and loading explanation before browser storage hydrates. Product Lab also publishes a manifest, robots file, sitemap, and intentional response headers.

## Hub comprehension

| Game | Hub promise | Time | Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Significant | Read experiment evidence. Spot the statistical trap. | 30 sec | Solo case |
| Ship It | Balance a quarter without crossing integrity lines. | 3 min | 12 decisions |
| Exception Room | Review AI exceptions before capacity runs out. | 8 min | 12 cases |

These descriptions state the playable mechanism instead of relying on a generic product tagline. They also make the three games distinguishable to a product leader or engineer scanning the portfolio.

## Shared interaction repair

- Each game card exposes one keyboard stop, the game link.
- The decorative Framer Motion drag wrapper has `tabIndex={-1}`.
- The drag surface uses `touch-action: pan-y`, so a vertical gesture can browse the hub.
- Drag remains bounded, snaps to origin, and does not replace the explicit play control.
- Settings exposes its expanded state and controlled panel relationship.
- Escape and outside click close the panel and restore trigger focus.
- Sound, Haptics, and Motion still persist across routes.

The first outside-click implementation used `pointerdown`. Browser testing caught that the later native click focus step could override focus restoration. The implementation now dismisses on document `click`, after the browser has completed its normal focus behavior.

## Public platform surface

- Root and game-specific metadata include canonical, Open Graph, and Twitter fields.
- Each game reuses its authored horizontal card artwork as the social preview image.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` are generated by the app.
- Direct entry responses contain a meaningful game heading and loading explanation before hydration.
- Response headers define content security, framing, referrer, content type, and unused capability policies.
- Analytics copy now states that Exception Room start events include the random seed for reproducibility.
- Ship It Daily copy states that its deck follows the device's local date.
