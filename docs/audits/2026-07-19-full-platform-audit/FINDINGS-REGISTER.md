# Product Lab findings register

Status: final reconciled register for the source audit, deterministic simulations, and 124 product interaction checks across the hub and three games. The separate 34-case engineering baseline is also complete.

## How to read this register

- `P1` means the issue can materially weaken comprehension, product credibility, completion, accessibility, or trust.
- `P2` means the issue creates meaningful friction, ambiguity, inconsistency, or regression risk.
- `P3` means the issue is limited to polish, resilience, or maintainability.
- `Source confirmed` means the behavior is directly established by the audited code or authored content.
- `Simulation confirmed` means the production decision engine produced the stated result in a deterministic diagnostic.
- `Browser confirmed` means fresh direct interaction reproduced the user-visible impact.
- `Browser not reproduced` means the source risk remains test-worthy but did not appear in the audited Chromium path.

The severities below are final for this audit scope. Evidence limits remain explicit in each product log.

Final count: 21 P1 findings, 38 P2 findings, and 2 P3 findings. The register also records 8 cross-product strengths to preserve.

## Browser reconciliation outcome

- All 124 hub and game interaction checks were attempted in the local production build.
- Supported paths were exercised with touch, pointer, keyboard, history, reload, storage, clipboard, motion preference, vibration instrumentation, and simulated 200 percent zoom as applicable.
- Source risks that did not reproduce are labeled as such instead of being promoted to browser-confirmed defects.
- The Exception Room engine error surface was not reachable through a supported interaction. Its presentation remains source-reviewed.
- Physical haptic output, physical speaker output, cross-platform emoji variance, crawler share-card rendering, and slow-hydration failure remain outside this local Chromium scope.
